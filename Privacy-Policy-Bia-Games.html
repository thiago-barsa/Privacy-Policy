Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bia Studio handles information collected through our mobile applications and games published on the Google Play Store.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our applications are designed to provide entertainment without the need for active collection of personally identifiable information. We do not request, collect, or store personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data from our users in these games.

2. Device Identifiers and Advertising

To provide our content for free, we use the Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use unique identifiers from your device (such as the Android Advertising ID - AAID) for the following purposes:

  • Displaying advertisements relevant to the user's profile.
  • Limiting the frequency of the same advertisement being shown.
  • Generating aggregated performance reports and fraud detection.

3. Third-Party Services

By using our applications, you interact with services provided by Google. We recommend reading the provider's privacy policy to understand how they handle technical data:

  • Google Privacy & Terms

4. Encryption and Security

While we do not collect sensitive data, we ensure that any technical data processed by integrated advertising SDKs is transmitted via secure and encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), following industry-standard security practices.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can perform immediate deletion.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
